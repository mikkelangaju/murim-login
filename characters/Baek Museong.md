# Baek Museong (백무성)

- **Safe through:** Chapter 328
- **Aliases:** Mount Hua’s Lone Crane
- **Gender:** male
- **Role:** First-generation disciple of the Mount Hua Sect and first of the Three Plum Blossom Elites, Baek Museong is widely expected to become the future Mount Hua Sect Leader and is accompanying Jin Taekyung's party to Tengwang Pavilion in Xi'an.
- **Personality:** Calm, responsible, principled, and patient, though visibly weary of his junior disciples’ antics.
- **Voice:** Gentle and polite with strangers; measured and stern when correcting junior disciples.
- **Relationships:** The current Mount Hua Sect Leader is his Master; Chulwoo and Eunhyang are his junior disciples; Cheongpung is his Martial Uncle through Mae Jonghak and the current Sect Leader; he met Cheongpung ten years ago; he has known Hyuk Sopyung since a Mount Hua–Zhongnan gathering ten years ago and remains one level above him.
- **Sources:** Korean source and accepted translation, Chapter 149
